Telling them apart

  • parser — the shared parser reads dates month first; our tenants write the day first. Day and month swap: 7 April arrives as 4 July. Every day-first tenant, every channel.
  • hint — booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type month first, as told, and the form reads the tenant’s way, day first. Only reports sent through the form, only tenants on booking-v3. Same symptom as the parser, different fix: the hint.
  • one-day move — the date moves by one day, or the weekday is off by one, or one meeting lands on different days in different cities. A swap cannot do that. Likely a time-zone conversion.
